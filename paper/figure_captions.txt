Figure 1. Three-gate MouseClaimBench workflow. The same claim, evidence blocks, and manifest determine the RDF/PROV-O representation and enter structural conformance S, domain authorization A, and integrity analysis I. Only the empty-set predicate I_clean enters the Boolean composition A* = S AND A AND I_clean. A failure at any gate prevents authorization, while the output preserves every violated structural, scientific, and integrity category with an actionable witness.

Figure 2. Complete profile-v2 claim-evidence matrix. Columns C1-C10 follow Table 3. A filled cell denotes a mandatory evidence block. The 60 filled cells are direct profile relations. In particular, C10 directly requires 21 blocks and does not inherit authorization from C1-C9.

Figure 3. False authorizations among 360 attacked packages across 10 profile claims, where lower values are better. The structural gate is excluded to isolate the domain-plus-integrity configurations. The profile-only baseline authorizes all 360 attacked packages. The hash-only baseline authorizes 280 packages involving identity, lineage, dependence, conflict, or provenance. The full integrity configuration blocks every declared attack. Seven original control omissions expose 10 packages each. Removing the contradiction check alone exposes none because block-attestation status consistency detects the same contradictory packages.
