\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The evaluation supports MouseClaimBench as a bounded \ac{kbs} for authorizing computational mouse-brain claims under an explicit evidence policy. The contribution is an inspectable representation of required evidence and a deterministic connection between source artifacts, rules, and dispositions. It is not evidence that the proposed profile is the unique or universally sufficient scientific taxonomy. Prediction, reproducibility, topology, direction, local observational structure--function association, intervention, and anatomical scope remain separate because they answer different questions.

\subsection{Interpretation and applicability}
\label{subsec:kbs-lessons}

The separation of profile and facts makes assumptions inspectable. The profile requires prediction, within-resource reproduction, topology, and direction for mechanism. Allen supplies facts for these blocks without case-specific inference code. Reproduction passes, while topology and direction fail, so mechanism is blocked. Non-compensation is decisive. The compensatory baseline improves sensitivity but increases false authorizations from 56 to 146. The prediction shortcut creates 785. Equation~\ref{eq:five-state-gate} prevents these substitutions while retaining ten supported real-case claims.

Proof traces also require two interpretations. Exact decisions, witnesses, and profile hashes establish faithful computation. They do not establish that every predicate is biologically sufficient or that a trace is useful to a human reviewer. Likewise, negative decisions are informative outputs. They identify the failed, absent, untargeted, or judgement-dependent requirement rather than hiding it in a global score.

Within-resource reproduction also covers heterogeneous designs. Cross-mouse agreement, split cohorts, and non-overlapping windows differ in evidential strength. MouseClaimBench preserves their subtype, sampling unit, and source observations rather than treating them as external replication. The engine can load another profile, but this is architectural extensibility rather than empirical generality. A new domain requires an independently specified vocabulary, predicates, priorities, and cases. Relative to knowledge graphs, claim verifiers, and assurance cases, MouseClaimBench performs the narrower task of provenance-bound claim authorization and can feed, but not replace, broader assurance workflows \citep{borrego2025research,wadden2020scifact,batarseh2021assurance}.

\subsection{Limitations and validity threats}
\label{subsec:limitations}

Construct validity depends on the author-proposed mouse-brain profile. The relation-level curation record makes its rationale and sources auditable, but no independent expert panel has assessed content validity and no consensus claim is made. The oracle reduces circularity because graphs and interventions are fixed before evidence predicates are executed. However, its five low-dimensional regimes do not cover arbitrary dynamics, noise levels, or sample sizes. Case-cluster bootstrap avoids treating ten decisions from one generated case as independent, but only five generator families are represented.

The four real cases are complementary audits rather than a representative sample. The 40 reference decisions were created by the authors during development and were neither blinded nor independently adjudicated. Their exact reproduction demonstrates migration fidelity only. The \ac{microns} endpoint is confined to one cortical volume. Its hold-outs provide within-resource reproduction rather than replication across animals. Unit-cluster bootstrap addresses neuron reuse, but network-dependent variance estimators and externally fixed endpoints remain necessary.

SciFact and Tuebingen are substitution controls rather than competitive evaluations. The proof trace has not been tested for human comprehensibility. This paper therefore does not claim reduced review time, improved agreement, or better decision quality. The exported structure is an operational explanation graph rather than a complete OWL, PROV-O, or general-purpose knowledge graph. The next evidential step is prospective evaluation on studies selected after freezing the profile, followed by independent expert assessment of relation validity and trace utility. These studies cannot be replaced by additional internal conformance tests.
