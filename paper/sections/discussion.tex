\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The evaluation supports MouseClaimBench as a bounded \ac{kbs} for authorizing computational mouse-brain claims under an explicit evidence policy. It connects source artifacts, required evidence, rules, and dispositions without asserting that the profile is a unique or universally sufficient taxonomy. Prediction, reproducibility, topology, direction, local structure--function association, intervention, and anatomical scope remain separate because they answer different questions.

\subsection{Interpretation and applicability}
\label{subsec:kbs-lessons}

The separation of profile and facts makes assumptions inspectable. The profile requires prediction, within-resource reproduction, topology, and direction for mechanism. Allen supplies facts for these blocks without case-specific inference code. Reproduction passes, while topology and direction fail, so mechanism is blocked. Non-compensation is decisive. The compensatory baseline improves sensitivity but increases false authorizations from 56 to 146. The prediction shortcut creates 785. Equation~\ref{eq:five-state-gate} prevents these substitutions while retaining ten supported real-case claims.

Exact decisions, witnesses, and profile hashes establish faithful computation. They do not establish that each predicate is biologically sufficient or that a trace is useful to a reviewer. Negative decisions remain informative because they identify the failed, absent, untargeted, or judgement-dependent requirement instead of hiding it in a global score.

Cross-mouse agreement, split cohorts, and non-overlapping windows differ in evidential strength. MouseClaimBench preserves their subtype and sampling unit rather than treating them as external replication. Loading another profile is architectural extensibility, not empirical generality. A new domain still requires an independently specified vocabulary, predicates, priorities, and cases. MouseClaimBench can feed, but not replace, broader assurance workflows \citep{borrego2025research,wadden2020scifact,batarseh2021assurance}.

\subsection{Limitations and validity threats}
\label{subsec:limitations}

Construct validity depends on the author-proposed profile. Its relation records are auditable, but no independent panel has assessed content validity. The oracle fixes graphs and interventions before executing evidence predicates, although five low-dimensional regimes cannot cover arbitrary dynamics, noise, or sample sizes. Case-cluster bootstrap preserves dependence among decisions, but only five generator families are represented.

The four real cases are complementary audits, not a representative sample. Their 40 decisions were created by the authors during development without blinding or independent adjudication, so exact reproduction demonstrates migration fidelity only. The \ac{microns} endpoint is confined to one cortical volume. Its hold-outs are within-resource reproduction. Unit-cluster bootstrap addresses neuron reuse, but network-dependent estimators and externally fixed endpoints remain necessary.

SciFact and Tuebingen are substitution controls, not competitive evaluations. Proof traces have not been tested for human comprehensibility, so this paper does not claim reduced review time, agreement, or decision quality. The exported structure is an operational explanation graph rather than a complete OWL, PROV-O, or general-purpose knowledge graph. Prospective evaluation after profile freeze and independent assessment of relation validity and trace utility remain necessary. More internal conformance tests cannot replace them.
