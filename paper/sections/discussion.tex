\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The results support MouseClaimBench as a bounded knowledge-based authorization layer. They do not support a claim that the system determines scientific truth. The system prevents evidence valid for one claim from being promoted into another that requires different evidence, using named requirements, package integrity, and category-complete deficit traces.

\subsection{Scientific interpretation and methodological novelty}
\label{subsec:interpretation-novelty}

External \ac{shacl} reproduces every structural decision and deficit. MouseClaimBench therefore claims no novelty for required-field validation, provenance representation, or JSON-LD export. Assurance cases already represent claims and evidence, and scientific verification retrieves documentary support. The contribution that remains is their evaluated composition with domain predicates, relational integrity, non-compensatory authorization, and unmasked deficit categories. Python and the separately implemented \ac{asp} path agree on domain outputs, \ac{shacl} permits scientifically failed but well-formed packages to conform, and the final object executes $S\land A\land I^{\mathrm{clean}}$ while retaining each layer's deficits.

The one-edge result is an expected monotonicity probe, not broad policy validation. Fifty-nine removals are not singly decisive because other deficits remain, while one removal exposes data quality as the sole Dynamic Sensorium barrier. The 71 contractions caused by added unprovided blocks do not establish that those additions are scientifically appropriate. Similarly, counterfactual repair establishes formal sufficiency, individual necessity, and witness retention, not human usefulness. The 41,641 deficits omitted by a one-reason baseline quantify information loss only.

The 2,550 attacked packages cover every composition of the eight historical families for every claim, but remain specification-driven. The nine-family suite and direct API regressions extend this controlled scope. All 20 coherent forgeries escape because synchronized false content, hashes, or independence metadata create no observable contradiction. The result identifies both controlled robustness and the trust boundary. It does not address digital signatures, archive compromise, adaptive attacks, or unmodelled dependence.

The prospective applications test protocol discipline rather than independent discovery. DANDI:000039 uses a deliberately simple Ridge model and author-defined criteria without established criterion validity. DANDI:001176 retains its availability refusal when only 5 of 29 listed subjects meet the frozen schema. The post-outcome sensitivity analysis exposes decision margins without replacing either rule. In \ac{microns}, dyadic covariance shows that naive pair-level inference is anti-conservative. Discovery fixes the direction, and both pre-fixed hold-outs confirm the local observational association. One cortical volume cannot establish a synaptic mechanism, external replication, or a complete visual-cortex model.

\paragraph{Lessons learned}\label{par:lessons-learned}
Four lessons follow. Structural conformance should use a standard engine. Provenance must record dependence claims, not only locations. Negative outcomes should remain release artifacts. Category-complete traces should retain every violated dimension and an actionable witness, but need not enumerate redundant occurrences. Their value to human reviewers remains unmeasured.

\subsection{Limitations and threats to validity}
\label{subsec:limitations}

Construct validity remains the principal limitation. Profile v2 is author-defined and literature-grounded, with inspectable claims, predicates, relations, rationales, exceptions, and sources. No independent panel has established their necessity, sufficiency, or completeness, and the frozen 92-item review protocol has zero eligible responses. Conformance, counterfactual fidelity, and perturbation are therefore profile-relative rather than evidence of scientific consensus.

Internal validity is stronger for software behaviour than scientific content. Expected mutation, counterfactual, and attack outcomes follow from the declared semantics. Multiple engines reduce implementation risk but share the profile specification. One-edge variants cover local monotonic behaviour, property checks sample finite packages, the attack generator is deterministic, and coherent false metadata remains unobservable. The DANDI sensitivity analysis is descriptive and provides no criterion validity.

The applications are heterogeneous rather than biological replications. Five retrospective packages come from MouseBrainBench \citep{fernandezisabel2026mousebrainbench}. The \ac{microns} windows share one volume, the DANDI studies use different measurements, DANDI:000039 tests bounded prediction rather than model superiority, and DANDI:001176 tests availability. Mouse-level bootstrap and dyadic covariance address declared dependence units but retain exchangeability assumptions. The hold-outs and frozen Git protocols were not registered externally.

External validity is not established outside computational mouse-brain evidence. Loading another profile is architectural extensibility, not empirical generality. Human comprehensibility and decision utility were not evaluated. Performance is limited to component microbenchmarks on one Apple arm64 host and excludes data access, source predicates, graph serialization, and external \ac{shacl} execution.
