\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

The evaluation is organized into four evidence classes. Knowledge acquisition asks whether the author-defined policy is fully traceable. Specification verification asks whether the implementation follows that policy and whether its explanations are counterfactually faithful. Controlled threat evaluation asks which declared integrity violations are detected and which coherent forgeries remain outside the trust model. Empirical application asks whether bounded decisions are preserved on existing and pre-access frozen mouse-brain resources. Performance is reported separately and is not used as scientific validation. Table~\ref{tab:experiment-design-v2} defines the evidence unit and maximum interpretation for every question.

\subsection{Experimental design and decision rules}
\label{subsec:experimental-design}

The acquisition audit compares the executable profile with its machine-readable basis and bibliography. Acceptance requires exact coverage of 10 claim definitions, 22 evidence blocks and predicate contracts, and 60 claim--evidence relations. Every relation must have a unique identifier and claim-specific necessity rationale. Every predicate must state evaluation ownership, decision-rule scope, required observations, sources, and exceptions. All source identifiers must resolve to the bibliography, and the audit must retain false values for independent content validity and human validation. The historical 29-item v1 panel packet is excluded. A separate v2 protocol contains 92 prospective review items covering the 10 claims, 22 predicates, and 60 relations. It requires at least seven eligible independent experts and predefined item content-validity, scale content-validity, agreement, and critical-veto conditions. It has zero completed independent raters and is not counted as evidence.

The contract benchmark generates eight case families from every profile claim. They contain pristine complete packages, one changed status, one omitted mandatory block, one omitted required observation, independent omission of source, rule, or rationale, and all pairwise combinations of the four non-passing states. For a claim with $q_c$ required blocks and $m_c$ mandatory observation fields across those blocks, the number of cases is
\begin{equation}
N_c=1+8q_c+m_c+16\binom{q_c}{2}.
\label{eq:contract-case-count}
\end{equation}
The terms represent one pristine package, four status mutations, one block omission, and three metadata omissions per block, one omission per observation field, and 16 status combinations per block pair. Table~\ref{tab:profile-v2-claims} reports every $N_c$, which sums to 5,677. Each case has an exact expected domain authorization and deficit set defined from Eqs.~\ref{eq:admissibility}--\ref{eq:core-authorization}. Python and the separately implemented \ac{asp} program evaluate all 5,677 domain cases. \ac{shacl} evaluates structural conformance and structural deficits over the same packages. Non-passing statuses remain structurally valid, while block, metadata, and required-observation omissions are structural failures. JSON-LD round-trip equivalence is evaluated through graph isomorphism. A separate truth-table benchmark evaluates all eight combinations of $S_{\Pi}$, $A_{\Pi}$, and integrity conformance against Eq.~\ref{eq:integrity-authorization}.

Domain-gate property-based verification uses 1,000 deterministic random evidence packages for each of the 10 claims. Every package is evaluated for soundness, completeness, exact deficits, input-order invariance, irrelevant-evidence invariance, and monotonic degradation whenever a passed block exists. Outside-profile closure is evaluated separately. Property-based tests generate additional status and observation mutations during the software suite. The reported 55,031 checks refer to the frozen deterministic experiment over $A_{\Pi}$, not to an exhaustive proof over arbitrary future profiles or complete package-integrity behaviour.

The one-edge structural policy perturbation holds 14 artifact instances fixed: the bounded target and strict complete-twin claim for each of five retrospective cases and two pre-access DANDI cases. It evaluates the baseline profile, all 60 profiles obtained by removing one required relation, and all 160 profiles obtained by adding one currently unrequired evidence block to one claim. Let $x_j=(c_j,B_j)$ denote one fixed claim and evidence package. For variant $\Pi_k$, the flip indicator is
\begin{equation}
F_{jk}=\mathrm{1}\!\left[A_{\Pi_k}(c_j,B_j)\neq
A_{\Pi}(c_j,B_j)\right].
\label{eq:profile-sensitivity-flip}
\end{equation}
Core authorization is used deliberately to isolate the scientific relation policy while manifests remain pristine and graphs remain structurally conformant. Removing a relation is a weakening perturbation and can only expand authorization. Adding a relation is a conservative extension and can only contract it when the added block is not admissibly passed. The resulting flips are therefore expected monotonicity probes under one-edge changes. They identify decisive relations in the fixed packages, but do not characterize broad policy dependence, scientific plausibility, or alternative expert profiles.

Counterfactual explanation fidelity is evaluated on another deterministic set of 1,000 packages per claim. Every non-authorized package is repaired by replacing exactly the reported deficits with complete passing attestations. The repaired package must authorize with no residual deficit. Each repaired block is then restored individually to its original defective state. Authorization must again fail with that block as the sole remaining deficit, implementing Eq.~\ref{eq:counterfactual-explanation}. Every witness must retain a non-empty block name, source, rule, and rationale. Finally, each of the 60 pristine claim--block relations is degraded independently. A single-reason baseline is evaluated descriptively by counting the simultaneous deficits it would omit. No claim about human usefulness is tested.

The original 370-case integrity benchmark evaluates the eight historical attack families identified in Table~\ref{tab:integrity-threat-model}. For each of the 10 claims, it contains one pristine package, every single attack, and all 28 pairwise combinations, yielding 360 attacked packages. The structural gate is excluded to isolate package-integrity controls, including manifest-level profile identity. For integrity configuration $k$, define the domain-plus-integrity decision
\begin{equation}
A^{I}_{\Pi,k}(c,B,M)=A_{\Pi}(c,B)
\mathrm{1}[I_{\Pi,k}(M,B)=\varnothing].
\label{eq:integrity-ablation-decision}
\end{equation}
Let $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{attack}}$ denote the benchmark cases, with claim $c_z$, blocks $B_z$, and manifest $M_z$ for case $z$. The configuration-specific attack false-authorization rate is
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{FAR}^{(k)}_{\mathrm{attack}}=
\frac{\sum_{z\in\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{attack}}}\mathrm{1}[z\text{ attacked}]
A^{I}_{\Pi,k}(c_z,B_z,M_z)}
{\sum_{z\in\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{attack}}}\mathrm{1}[z\text{ attacked}]}.
\label{eq:attack-far}
\end{equation}
We compare the complete domain-plus-integrity configuration with the core profile alone, a hash-only baseline, and eight leave-one-control-out configurations. Exact trace recovery requires the detected deficit codes to equal every invariant violated by the applied attacks. Full $S_{\Pi}\land A_{\Pi}\land I_{\Pi}$ composition is evaluated separately by the eight-state truth-table experiment. Consequently, Table~\ref{tab:integrity-ablation-v2} measures failures of integrity configurations rather than failures of the final authorization object. The compositional stress test evaluates all $2^8-1=255$ non-empty subsets of the same historical families for each claim, producing 2,550 attacked packages. A separate nine-family extended suite contains 10 pristine and 90 targeted reference- or attestation-level regression packages. Six direct API edge-case regressions, outside that suite, cover duplicate identifiers, reflexive relations, and both branches of the study-scoped data-generation identity rule. Finally, 20 trust-boundary controls replace content and hashes coherently or forge internally consistent independence metadata. Their expected authorization exposes the need for external trust anchors. None of these tests is an adaptive security guarantee.

The core authorization-engine microbenchmark is a component test measured on one Apple arm64 host with Python 3.12.13. Pristine authorization batches contain 100, 1,000, and 10,000 packages drawn cyclically from all profile claims. Each batch is repeated five times after 100 warm-up decisions. A separate timed component expands one valid digital-twin package to 25, 100, 1,000, and 5,000 uniquely content-addressed artifacts and evaluates its local integrity gate. Median wall-clock time and all repetitions are stored. Downloads, source-predicate execution, graph serialization, and external \ac{shacl} validation are excluded. These are component microbenchmarks on one implementation and host, not end-to-end scalability or a portable latency guarantee.

Three artifact-evaluation components complement the controlled benchmarks. The first reuses five result packages assembled during the preceding MouseBrainBench study \citep{fernandezisabel2026mousebrainbench} and asks a different question: whether profile v2 authorizes their bounded target claim and strict digital-twin claim. These mappings are retrospective and are not counted as prospective validation. The second applies dependence-aware inference to \ac{microns}. Its endpoint was fixed after discovery and evaluated in two non-overlapping hold-out windows within one tissue volume. Directed pair regression controls log soma distance, its square, pre- and postsynaptic degree, and coarse cell type. Dependence is addressed with directed dyadic cluster covariance and 1,000 simultaneous node-label Freedman--Lane permutations. These hold-outs provide internal reproduction, not independent biological replication. The third component evaluates the two external \ac{dandi} protocols described below.

For the \ac{microns} permutation test, the full model regresses readout-location similarity $y_{ij}$ on the directed connection indicator $c_{ij}$ and the fixed control vector $z_{ij}$:
\begin{equation}
y_{ij}=\beta_0+\beta_1c_{ij}+z_{ij}^{\top}\gamma+\varepsilon_{ij}.
\label{eq:microns-full-model}
\end{equation}
The reduced model omits $c_{ij}$. Its residuals are placed in a directed neuron-by-neuron array. The connection indicator is residualized against the same controls, and the residualized coefficient is the test statistic. Each permutation draws one permutation of neuron identifiers and applies it simultaneously to sender and receiver indices of the reduced-model residual array. This preserves reciprocal array structure and the incidence pattern induced by shared neurons under relabelling. The discovery window fixes the positive direction and the analysis specification. Its one-sided permutation value is reported descriptively and has no confirmatory interpretation. The dyadic regression is reported with a two-sided test and 95\% interval in every window. The confirmatory one-sided permutation test is subsequently evaluated in the two pre-fixed hold-outs. Its finite-permutation value is $(1+K)/(1+R)$, where $K$ is the number of permuted coefficients at least as large as the observed positive coefficient and $R=1000$. Discovery and the two hold-outs use reproducible streams with seeds 2026080201, 2026080202, and 2026080203. A hold-out passes only when $\widehat{\beta}_1>0$, $p_{\mathrm{dyadic}}\leq0.05$, and $p_{\mathrm{perm}}\leq0.05$. Confirmation requires this conjunction in both fixed hold-outs after a positive discovery direction. This procedure assumes exchangeability of the reduced-model residual array under simultaneous node relabelling.

Two external \ac{dandi} protocols were committed before numerical asset access. The availability protocol was frozen in commit \texttt{b59a497} and the predictive protocol in \texttt{d039e66}. Their SHA-256 identities are retained in the result artifact, and the numerical result was committed later. This Git history is temporal evidence for the declared ordering, not third-party preregistration. DANDI:001176 was selected from metadata describing 29 subjects and simultaneous cholinergic axon, cortical \ac{ach}, pupil, and treadmill signals \citep{neyhart2024ach}. The protocol required at least 20 usable subjects and prohibited threshold or endpoint repair. After catalog inspection, 28 schema-valid sessions came from only 5 subjects. The predictive model was therefore not executed.

DANDI:000039 contains contrast-tuning recordings associated with a published visual-cortex study \citep{millman2020vip}. Before inspecting its asset catalog or values, the protocol fixed one lexicographically first \texttt{behavior+ophys.nwb} asset per subject, a trial-level population mean event response, Ridge regression with $\alpha=1$, and an intercept comparator. For contrast $q$ and direction $\theta$ in degrees, the exact five regressors are $\log(1+100q)$, $\sin(\theta)$, $\cos(\theta)$, $\log(1+100q)\sin(\theta)$, and $\log(1+100q)\cos(\theta)$. Angular functions are evaluated after conversion to radians. The first 60\% of trials train the model, the next 20\% remain reserved, and the final 20\% form the test partition. The intermediate partition is deliberately unused. It does not affect model fitting, model selection, threshold definition, or final evaluation. The \texttt{StandardScaler} is fitted on the training partition only and then applied unchanged to the held-out partition. Ridge $\alpha=1$ is fixed and no tuning occurs after asset access. The per-mouse held-out metric is Pearson correlation. Authorization requires at least 25 subjects, median held-out correlation at least 0.10, a positive 95\% mouse-bootstrap lower bound, at least 65\% positive subjects, and trial-pooled mean squared error below the intercept. For $M$ mice with $n_m$ held-out trials, this metric is
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{MSE}_{\mathrm{pool}}=
\frac{\sum_{m=1}^{M}\sum_{t=1}^{n_m}(y_{mt}-\widehat{y}_{mt})^2}
{\sum_{m=1}^{M}n_m}.
\label{eq:dandi-pooled-mse}
\end{equation}
The implementation stores and compares the two squared-error numerators because their denominator is identical. The resulting metric weights mice in proportion to their number of held-out trials. All conditions must pass. The bootstrap uses protocol seed 2026080202 and resamples mice with replacement 5,000 times. Each replicate calculates the median mouse-level Pearson correlation. The reported lower bound is its 2.5th percentile. It therefore targets population stability across mice rather than trial-level independence.

These thresholds are author-defined operational acceptance criteria rather than criterion-validated biological standards. Twenty-five mice was intended to preserve a multi-mouse endpoint and a meaningful mouse-level bootstrap, but it was not obtained from a formal power calculation. The 0.10 median correlation requires a non-trivial predictive signal, the positive lower bound requires population-level sign stability, the 65\% rule requires a majority of positive mice while tolerating heterogeneity, and the intercept comparison requires incremental predictive value. The 20-subject availability boundary for DANDI:001176 similarly protects the intended cross-subject design but is not a sufficiency standard. A post-outcome sensitivity analysis changes one threshold at a time while holding the other frozen conditions fixed. It exposes decision boundaries and cannot retrospectively calibrate or replace either protocol.

\subsection{Knowledge traceability, one-edge policy perturbation, and explanation fidelity}
\label{subsec:construct-validity-results}

The acquisition audit exactly covers the 10 claims, 22 evidence blocks, 22 predicate contracts, 60 claim--evidence relations, and 124 required observation slots. All 60 relation identifiers and necessity rationales are unique, and all 20 source identifiers resolve to the bibliography. The ledger separates upstream predicate execution from authorization checks and marks every relation as a source-grounded author requirement without independent consensus. This satisfies traceability of the current policy. It does not establish that the policy is complete or optimal.

The one-edge analysis evaluates 221 profiles and 3,094 profile--case combinations. The baseline authorizes four of the 14 fixed decisions. Among the 60 weakening variants, only removal of the \texttt{data\_quality} requirement from bounded prediction changes an authorization. It converts the Dynamic Sensorium target from a documented quality refusal into an authorization. This isolates the requirement that is decisive for that case rather than showing that it should be removed. Among 160 conservative extensions, 52 variants change at least one decision and produce 71 contractions. These contractions follow the non-compensatory monotonicity of the system because an otherwise authorized case has no admissible attestation for the newly added block. Ninety-six variants change at least one deficit set even when authorization does not change. The counts are implementation context, not independent scientific evidence for the added relations.

\input{tables/profile_sensitivity_v2}

Counterfactual fidelity is evaluated on 10,000 packages. Of the 9,839 non-authorized packages, repairing every reported deficit is sufficient for authorization with no residual deficit. Restoring each deficit individually produces 51,480 minimality checks, all of which refuse authorization with the exact retained deficit. The same 51,480 deficit records retain non-empty witnesses, and all 60 pristine single-block degradations return the expected sole deficit. There are 8,632 packages with simultaneous deficits. A single-reason trace would retain 9,839 deficits and therefore omit the remaining $51{,}480-9{,}839=41{,}641$. This is arithmetic information retention under the generated packages, not independent evidence about explanation quality or human decisions.

\subsection{Domain-gate conformance and property-based verification results}
\label{subsec:conformance-results}

Table~\ref{tab:standards-formal-results} reports the principal conformance results. The Python engine produces no false authorization or false rejection in 5,677 cases and recovers every expected domain deficit set. The independent \ac{asp} implementation matches Python domain status and deficits in all 5,677 cases while deriving admissibility from declared field-presence facts inside Clingo. The \ac{shacl} engine classifies the same 5,677 packages against their structural expectations and returns every expected structural deficit. In particular, status-only failures remain structurally conformant and scientifically unauthorized. All eight logical combinations of structural conformance, domain authorization, and integrity conformance produce the final decision required by Eq.~\ref{eq:integrity-authorization}. The exported profile contains 318 \ac{rdf} triples representing all 10 claims and 22 evidence blocks. JSON-LD serialization and parsing preserve graph isomorphism in five representative packages: a pristine package, a scientifically failed but structurally conformant package, a structurally incomplete package, a rich manifest with multiple attestations, and a larger digital-twin-style package with declared independence and cohort-disjointness relations.

\input{tables/standards_formal_results}

These results separate two conclusions. Python and \ac{asp} agree on domain authorization and scientific deficits. External \ac{shacl} validation agrees with the independently declared structural expectations rather than with domain decisions. It does not recompute source predicates or relational integrity. Structural completeness and structural deficit reporting are therefore not unique capabilities of MouseClaimBench. The additional contribution must be assessed through the explicit non-compensatory composition, domain requirements, package integrity, controlled attacks, and real applications.

The 10,000 deterministic random packages produce 55,031 domain-gate property checks with no violation. Soundness, completeness, deficit identity, input-order invariance, and irrelevant-evidence invariance each pass 10,000 checks. Monotonic degradation passes all 5,030 applicable checks. Outside-profile closure also passes. These results establish the operational semantics of $A_{\Pi}$ for profile v2. They do not establish final-package invariance or prove that the evidence requirements are scientifically complete.

\subsection{Integrity attacks, ablation, and component microbenchmarks}
\label{subsec:integrity-results}

Under the declared deterministic threat model, the complete domain-plus-integrity configuration returns the exact integrity-deficit trace in all 370 packages. It produces no false rejection on the 10 pristine packages and no false authorization on the 360 attacked packages. The core profile does not inspect package integrity and therefore authorizes all 360 attacked packages. Hash checking alone blocks content tampering but authorizes 280 packages affected by profile substitution, broken or cyclic lineage, false independence, cohort overlap, contradictory attestations, or missing block provenance. The structural gate is excluded from these counts, so manifest-level profile substitution remains visible instead of being rejected earlier by structural identity matching.

\input{figures/integrity_ablation_v2_figure}

The leave-one-out result distinguishes unique necessity from defensive overlap. Removing any of seven original controls creates 10 false authorizations, corresponding to the single-attack package for that omitted control in each claim. Removing only the contradictory-attestation check creates none because the same package also violates block--attestation status consistency. Pairwise attacks containing another enabled control remain blocked. Table~\ref{tab:integrity-ablation-v2} gives the exact values, while Fig.~\ref{fig:integrity-ablation-v2} shows the contrast between incomplete baselines and the complete domain-plus-integrity configuration. The result demonstrates conditional necessity and one explicit redundancy under the declared attack construction. It does not imply sufficiency for every real provenance threat.

\input{tables/integrity_ablation_v2}

The compositional stress test evaluates 2,560 packages, comprising 10 pristine packages and 2,550 attacked packages. For each claim, the latter span all 255 non-empty compositions of the eight historical attack families. The complete domain-plus-integrity configuration produces no false authorization and returns every implied invariant deficit. The extended nine-family regression suite returns exact traces across its 100 packages, with no false authorization among 90 attacked packages and no false rejection among 10 pristine packages. The six separate direct API regressions return structured refusals for duplicate and reflexive declarations and the expected pass or refusal under the composite generation-identity rule. All 20 internally coherent forgeries remain authorized because no externally verifiable contradiction exists. The system therefore detects inconsistency with declared invariants, but it does not authenticate self-consistent source metadata.

Batch throughput ranges from 27,653 to 52,816 authorizations per second. Median time is 0.00189 s for 100 packages, 0.03253 s for 1,000, and 0.36163 s for 10,000. A valid package requires 0.149 ms with 25 artifacts, 0.240 ms with 100, and 5.328 ms with 5,000. These measurements describe the observed range on one host and are not an asymptotic complexity result. All pristine decisions remain authorized at every scale.

\subsection{Mouse-brain artifacts and prospective external applications}
\label{subsec:application-results}

The retrospective profile-v2 mapping contains five target claims and the corresponding five strict digital-twin claims. Three bounded target claims are authorized. Static Sensorium authorizes prediction for its declared five-dataset comparison. The \ac{ibl} case authorizes topology-specific behavioural prediction under its locked comparison. \ac{microns} authorizes one local observational structure--function association. Allen directed topology remains unauthorized because prediction, topology, direction, uncertainty, robustness, and data-quality requirements do not all pass. Dynamic Sensorium remains unauthorized because the stored response-reliability quality block requires review. None of the five cases authorizes the strict digital-twin claim.

The dependence-aware \ac{microns} analysis changes the status of the local association without changing its scope. Table~\ref{tab:microns-dyadic-results} reports cohort size, pair counts, effect estimates, uncertainty, and both inferential checks. No permuted statistic is at least as large as the observed positive statistic in any window. With 1,000 permutations, the finite-permutation corrected value is therefore $p=1/1001\approx0.001$ in each window. The discovery value is descriptive because that window selected the positive direction. Both pre-fixed hold-outs independently pass the positive coefficient, two-sided dyadic, and one-sided permutation criteria. Shared-neuron dependence is addressed more conservatively than naive pair-level inference. All windows nevertheless come from one \ac{microns} volume, and readout-location similarity is a derived functional descriptor rather than a causal measurement.

\input{tables/microns_dyadic_results}

The two prospective DANDI outcomes are deliberately asymmetric. DANDI:001176 fails the frozen availability condition with 5 usable subjects against a minimum of 20. No predictive fit is reported, and the endpoint is not weakened after seeing the catalog. DANDI:000039 yields 32 selected and usable mice. Its median held-out correlation is 0.310, the mouse-bootstrap 95\% interval is $[0.207,0.369]$, and 93.75\% of subjects have positive correlation. The stored model and intercept squared-error sums are 11.579 and 12.244 over 6,193 held-out trials. They correspond to trial-pooled mean squared errors of 0.001870 and 0.001977, giving the same relative reduction of 5.43\%. All five frozen acceptance conditions pass, so the system authorizes only bounded population-response prediction within this resource. A later descriptive range-read audit verifies that finite contrast and direction counts match the frozen trial counts for all 32 mice. Every held-out contrast--direction combination and every held-out trial condition occurs in the corresponding training partition. No mouse has a test-only contrast, direction, or contrast--direction combination. This audit does not alter the split, model, thresholds, or authorization.

\input{tables/dandi_threshold_sensitivity}

The one-at-a-time sensitivity results preserve the positive decision at the frozen DANDI:000039 thresholds and expose finite margins. Within the declared grids, the decision fails when the subject requirement exceeds 32, the median-correlation threshold reaches 0.35, the bootstrap-lower-bound requirement reaches 0.25, the positive-subject requirement reaches 0.95, or a minimum 7.5\% relative squared-error improvement is added. DANDI:001176 remains unavailable for every tested requirement of at least 10 subjects. It would pass availability only if the minimum were relaxed to the observed five subjects, which is exactly the outcome-informed repair prohibited by the frozen protocol. These post-outcome checks describe sensitivity around two realized outcomes. They provide no criterion validity for the original values.
