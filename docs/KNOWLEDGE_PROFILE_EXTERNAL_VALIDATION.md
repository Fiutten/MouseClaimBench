# External validation of the knowledge profile

The current profile is author-proposed. Structural documentation and executable
tests do not establish content validity. Independent experts must judge whether
each evidence-to-claim relation and inference rule is relevant, comprehensive,
comprehensible, and scientifically safe.

The frozen study requires at least seven completed independent reviewers, five
years of relevant experience, and at least two reviewers covering each of three
expertise strata. Authors and system developers cannot count as independent.
Conflicts and non-response must be reported.

The packet contains 22 evidence-to-claim relations, five non-compensatory rules,
and two profile-level comprehensiveness items. Each expert scores relevance,
comprehensibility, and scientific safety on a four-point scale and may issue a
written critical veto. Acceptance requires every item CVI to reach 0.78, each
scale-average CVI to reach 0.90, binary Fleiss kappa to reach 0.60, and no
unresolved critical veto.

These thresholds are design decisions grounded in content-validity and
consensus-reporting methods. They do not guarantee universal scientific
agreement. Failed items require a documented revision and a new independent
round. Until real ratings pass the executable audit, repository and manuscript
language must remain `author-proposed, not externally content-validated`.

Methodological references include COSMIN content-validity methodology
(doi:10.1007/s11136-018-1829-0), Lynn's CVI framework (PMID:3640358), and the
ACCORD consensus-reporting guideline (doi:10.1371/journal.pmed.1004326).
