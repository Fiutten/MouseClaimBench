# Mouse-Brain Claim Profile Curation

## Scientific status

The executable mouse-brain profile is an author-proposed, literature-grounded
operational policy. Its logical consistency, relation coverage, execution
semantics, and behaviour on selected synthetic and artifact-grounded cases are
tested. It has not been validated as a community consensus by an independent
expert panel.

## Acquisition record

The profile was assembled through a targeted synthesis of five source families:

1. knowledge acquisition and rule-system validation
2. computational reproducibility and external replication
3. model verification, validation, uncertainty, and assurance
4. causal and directional identification
5. mouse-brain prediction, connectivity, simulation, and digital-twin resources

The machine-readable curation record is stored in
`mousebrainbench/knowledge/profiles/mouse_brain_claims_v1_basis.yaml`. Every
claim-to-evidence relation records its operational role, rationale, scope,
exceptions, rejected substitution, and source identifiers. Automated validation
requires exact coverage of the executable relation set.

## Interpretation

Requirements are necessary under this profile. They are not asserted to be a
unique or scientifically sufficient taxonomy. A `supported` result authorizes a
claim only within the declared profile, protocol, and provenance boundary.
External expert elicitation, adjudication, and prospective evaluation on studies
selected after profile freeze remain necessary before any consensus-validity
claim.

## Change control

Any change to claim vocabulary, required evidence, relation rationale, or rule
priority requires:

1. a profile version increment
2. a matching curation record
3. complete relation-coverage tests
4. regeneration of profile-bound artifacts
5. explicit reporting of changed decisions
