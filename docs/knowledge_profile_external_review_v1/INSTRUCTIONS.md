# Independent review instructions

Review every row in `review_items.csv` in the item order assigned from your
anonymous `rater_id`. Do not consult other panel members during round 1.

For each item, enter a score from 1 to 4 for relevance, comprehensibility, and
scientific safety in `ratings.csv`. Choose `retain`, `revise`, or `remove`. A
critical veto means that retaining the item could authorize a materially unsafe
or invalid scientific inference. Every veto requires a concrete rationale.

Complete one row in `raters.csv`. Expertise fields and independence statements
are required. Public reports will use anonymous codes and aggregate expertise.
Declared conflicts are reported and adjudicated before analysis.

The authors must not alter ratings. Corrections are submitted as a new complete
file with an audit record. Missing items, duplicate responses, ineligible panel
composition, or fewer than seven independent completed raters produce a pending
decision rather than an imputed result.

Round 1 disagreement is preserved. If revision is required, changes and reasons
are documented before a separately versioned round 2. A consensus score must
not erase minority safety objections.
