# Direction router v4.2 prospective repair

The frozen v4 router result remains negative because three of 540 independent
cases passed its OR-combined association screen. Version 4.2 is a new experiment,
not a reinterpretation of v4. It uses a new seed namespace and eight new
structural-equation regimes.

The repair requires both Pearson and Spearman tests to show absolute association
of at least 0.10 after a two-test Bonferroni correction at familywise alpha
0.001. This conservative conjunction is only a routing precondition. It does
not identify direction and cannot convert observational output into causal
evidence.

Success requires zero attempts in independent regimes, at least 0.80 accuracy
among attempted directions, at least 0.80 accuracy on valid routed regimes, and
at least 0.80 coverage of valid routes. All criteria and the 2,160-case design
were fixed before v4.2 generation.
