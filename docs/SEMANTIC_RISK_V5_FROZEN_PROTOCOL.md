# Semantic risk v5: frozen protocol

## Purpose

Version 5 addresses the three unresolved items reported by the v4 release audit.
It adds a new prospective population, makes the independent statistical unit
explicit, and prepares rather than fabricates external content validation.

The confirmatory block uses the published TimeGraph generators. The official
source is executed after two non-numerical compatibility transformations. Colab
shell magic and an unused plotting import are removed. The equations, random
draws, coefficients, state updates, and returned data are not reimplemented.

## Inferential unit

Claims share variables and observations. Scenarios also share a fixed generator
family. The primary unit is therefore a seed bundle. A bundle fails when any
claim authorized anywhere inside it is false. Exact binomial bounds are computed
only over seed bundles. Pair-level and scenario-level results are descriptive.

Fifty bundles are fixed for target calibration, fifty for the risk lock, and
fifty for final evaluation. Final outcomes may be evaluated only if the risk lock
passes risk, activation, recovery, semantic-integrity, and minimum-unit checks.

## External transport

CausalRivers is a real hydrological benchmark with two large regional graphs and
one flood subset. Its many sampled pairs are not independent biological or
regional replications. It is used to test frozen-policy transport and shift
warnings. It cannot establish an exact external risk guarantee.

## Prospective status

Before this freeze, source code, documentation, licenses, release size, and one
TimeGraph CSV format preview were inspected. No v5 outcome statistic was
computed. Consequently, the generated seed bank is prospective, whereas the
public pre-generated TimeGraph suite is not treated as a blind hold-out.

## Content validity

The authors can test schema completeness and deterministic execution. They
cannot constitute independent domain experts for their own profile. Version 5
will produce a frozen external-review packet and analysis code. The profile must
remain labelled author-proposed until independent reviewers provide judgments.

## Non-compensation

A successful synthetic certificate cannot turn CausalRivers into independent
replication. A shift warning cannot restore a failed certificate. Predictive
accuracy cannot compensate for missing directed or interventional evidence. No
aggregate score can override these rules.
