# Semantic risk control v4.1 final lock

The original v4 external contract failed before final evaluation. Its global
policy could satisfy the risk bound only through total abstention. The
`target_calibration` and `risk_lock` roles are consequently treated as consumed
development data. Their negative v4 result remains immutable.

Before reading any CSV value from the `final_evaluation` role, v4.1 fixes a
claim-family selection rule and a joint threshold. Separate development
calibration retained only `predictive` and `internally_reproduced`. The four
stronger families had no non-degenerate certificate and are excluded. The joint
development policy uses the semantic gate and threshold 1.0. Neither family set,
threshold, score model, nor evidence profile may change after this lock.

The final endpoint remains the stringent experiment-level event used by v4. A
single false authorization makes an experiment fail. The exact interval is
conditional on experiment-level exchangeability. The evaluation also reports
dataset-stratified descriptive sensitivity because experiments within one
dataset may retain dependence.

A positive result supports only bounded predictive and internal-reproduction
authorization in this physical-system population. It cannot support topology,
direction, mechanism, causality, universal transfer, or mouse-brain biological
validity.
