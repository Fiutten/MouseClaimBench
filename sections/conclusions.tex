\section{Conclusions and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusions}

MouseClaimBench provides an integrity-aware knowledge-based method for authorizing bounded scientific claims from computational mouse-brain evidence. Its authorization contracts jointly bind graph conformance, package integrity, domain evidence semantics, and complete non-compensatory deficits. Authorization requires every mandatory block and every integrity control to pass. The result is tied to a named profile and includes all deficits, witnesses, artifact identities, and protocol revisions.

The revised evaluation separates acquisition, specification verification, controlled threats, and empirical application. The acquisition ledger covers all 60 claim--evidence relations and 22 predicate contracts without claiming external consensus. Python and \ac{shacl} agree on all 5,497 structural cases, while the independent \ac{asp} path agrees on its 262-case deterministic subset. Structural sensitivity evaluates 221 profiles and 3,094 fixed artifact decisions. One relation removal expands one authorization, while 52 conservative extensions create 71 contractions. Counterfactual repair and minimality checks show no violation across 9,839 repaired packages and 51,480 individual deficits. These results quantify policy dependence and explanation fidelity. They do not validate the scientific completeness of the policy.

The integrity results now cover all 2,550 non-empty compositions of eight declared attacks with exact traces and no false authorization. The 20 coherent-forgery controls all remain authorized, which exposes rather than conceals the unresolved need for external trust anchors. The scientific applications preserve the same boundaries. One prospective DANDI protocol remains negative because its frozen subject requirement is unavailable. A second authorizes only population-response prediction across 32 mice. Dependence-aware \ac{microns} inference authorizes one local observational structure--function association. No evaluated case authorizes a causal mechanism, external biological replication, whole-brain coverage, or a complete entity-specific mouse-brain digital twin.

The next scientific requirement is independent assessment of the 92 v2 claim, predicate, and relation items, followed by evaluation of whether complete traces improve human review decisions. The integrity model should add signed attestations, archive trust policies, and adaptive attacks, while future external endpoints should be registered across laboratories and animals. New domain profiles require their own acquisition record, sensitivity analysis, external cases, and content-validation process. The immediate conclusion remains narrower and testable: predictive accuracy, reproducible execution, and valid graph structure cannot compensate for missing scientific evidence or incoherent provenance.
